K-Agent Android privacy policy draft

Not ready for Google Play submission. Release hosting, model routing, and log retention are not yet confirmed. This draft describes the current app and gateway code and must be checked against the actual deployment before release. The proposed effective date is November 19, 2026.

Application and contact

K-Agent for Android (com.kensly.kagent) lets you send a conversation to a configured gateway and receive model-generated responses. The developer is bighartedbon. Contact: efroidissement@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

The app stores the conversation and the gateway secret in encrypted storage on your device. It sends the entire saved conversation and the gateway secret to the configured gateway when you request a response. Release builds require an HTTPS gateway connection.

The app excludes its data from Android backup. You can delete the saved conversation using New conversation; clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app also deletes its locally stored data, including the gateway secret. These actions do not necessarily delete information held by a gateway, hosting service, or model provider.

Gateway and model processing

The current gateway sends conversation messages to its configured Ollama service for model inference. Cloud fallback is off by default. If you enable Allow Gemini fallback and local inference fails, the gateway may send the full submitted conversation to Google's Gemini API. Google processes that information under the terms applicable to the gateway operator's Gemini configuration.

The gateway's audit log records request metadata including the client IP address, timestamp, request method and path, response status, and duration. Chat turn telemetry records timing, status, a trace identifier, and, when available, the model provider. Provider error or malformed response bodies may also be included in gateway error logs. Hosting or network services may collect additional information. Retention periods depend on the release deployment and have not yet been confirmed.

The developer states that personal data is not sold. To request access to or deletion of information held outside your device, contact the address above; the intended response time is within 30 days.

Decisions required before publication as a release policy

  • Confirm the release gateway operator, hosting provider, location, and all providers that process or store data. Google Cloud Run and Gemini have been proposed, but the release deployment was also described as undecided.
  • Verify the actual model routing, including where Ollama runs and whether Gemini or any other services are enabled.
  • Verify what the deployed gateway, model services, and hosting provider retain, including chat content and logs, and document retention periods and deletion procedures.
  • Verify encryption for connections between the deployed gateway and model services, and that provider terms match the actual configuration.
  • Confirm the developer's public legal identity, access and deletion procedures, and effective date.
