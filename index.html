Privacy Policy

App: K-Agent A.I. Engineer (io.bighartedbon.kagent)
Developer / Operator: bighartedbon
Effective Date: November 19, 2026

K-Agent A.I. Engineer ("the App") lets you send conversations to a configured gateway and receive model-generated responses. This Privacy Policy explains how bighartedbon ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the App and its release gateway.

1. Information Used by the App

  • On-Device Storage: The App stores your active conversation and gateway secret in encrypted storage on your device and excludes its data from Android cloud backup.
  • Data Sent on Request: When you request a response, the App sends the saved conversation and gateway secret over HTTPS to the configured gateway.
  • No Ads or Accounts: The App does not require user account registration, does not include third-party advertising SDKs, and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

2. Gateway Hosting & Model Processing

  • Hosting Provider: The release gateway is operated by bighartedbon and hosted on Google Cloud Run (europe-west2).
  • Model Routing: In the public Cloud Run release, submitted conversation messages are routed over HTTPS/TLS to the Google Gemini API for model inference (subject to Google Cloud and Gemini API terms). Local Ollama inference is disabled in the public Cloud Run release and applies only if you self-host your own gateway.
  • Additional Tools: If optional tools (such as Google Maps) are invoked, only the query parameters needed for that request are transmitted.

3. Operational Logs & Telemetry

To maintain security, reliability, and diagnostics, the gateway records:

  • Audit Logs: Client IP address, timestamp, HTTP request method and path, response status, and request duration.
  • Chat Turn Telemetry: Timing, status, trace identifier, and model provider name.
  • Error Logs: If a provider error or malformed response occurs, error details may be logged for troubleshooting.

Conversation contents are processed transiently to generate responses and are not stored in a persistent user database operated by us. Standard Cloud Run operational logs are retained temporarily (typically up to 30 days) by the hosting infrastructure and automatically purged.

4. Data Selling & Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information is shared only with the hosting and model providers listed above strictly to operate the service, or when required by law.

5. Security

Release builds require an HTTPS/TLS connection between the App and the gateway, and connections between the gateway and external model/tool providers also use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

6. Your Controls, Retention & Deletion

  • On-Device Deletion: You can delete your saved conversation at any time using New conversation inside the App, or delete all local data (including the gateway secret) by clearing App data in Android settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Off-Device Access & Deletion: Clearing on-device data does not automatically erase temporary server logs or data processed by external model providers. To request access to or deletion of off-device records held by us, email efroidissement@gmail.com. Verified requests are handled within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a developer and engineering tool not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes & Contact

Updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For any privacy questions or deletion requests, contact bighartedbon at efroidissement@gmail.com.
